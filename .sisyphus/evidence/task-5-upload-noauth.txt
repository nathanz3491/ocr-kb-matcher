QA Scenario 1: Unauthenticated POST to /api/upload
====================================================
Command: curl -X POST http://localhost:3001/api/upload

Response:
HTTP/1.1 401 Unauthorized
Body: {"success":false,"error":"No authorization header provided"}

NOTE: The 'authenticate' middleware (applied via router.use at line 36) catches
unauthenticated requests before they reach the route-level 'requireAuth' and
'enforceQuota' middlewares. This is expected — the auth gate prevents the quota
middleware from ever being reached by unauthenticated callers.

Expected: 401 AUTH_REQUIRED (authenticate returns 401 No authorization header)
