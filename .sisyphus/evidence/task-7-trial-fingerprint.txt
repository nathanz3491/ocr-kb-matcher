Task 7 — Free trial abuse prevention: Device fingerprint-based blocking

Device fingerprint: SHA-256 hash of `userAgent + acceptLanguage`
Example: crypto.createHash('sha256').update('Mozilla/5.0 en-US').digest('hex')
