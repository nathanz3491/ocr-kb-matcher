## Progressive Delay
- 3rd failed attempt → 1s server-side delay before 401 response
- 5th failed attempt → 5s server-side delay
- 10th failed attempt → account locked for 1 hour
- Delay applied BEFORE response, making brute-forcing time-prohibitive

## Lockout Behavior
- After 10 failed attempts → account locked for 3600 seconds
- Locked accounts return HTTP 429 with Retry-After header
- Both wrong AND correct passwords are rejected while locked
- Lock cleared after 1 hour (lockedUntil expires)
